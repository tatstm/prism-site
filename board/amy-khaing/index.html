About

Amy Khaing is a member of the Editorial Board at PRISM and a senior at Milton Academy who is interested in journalism, social justice, and global affairs.

At Milton, Amy is the Editor-in-Chief of the Yearbook, an organizer for TEDx, and co-head of the History Club and Christian Fellowship. In her free time, she loves making art, watching movies, and hanging out with friends. Amy is excited for PRISM to be a platform where students can showcase their research projects!
